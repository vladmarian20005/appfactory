# Quizday, launch schedule

Quizday has been on the App Store since **29 September 2026**
(https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quizday-daily-trivia-no-ads/id6810429464), so "approval day"
has already passed. This schedule counts from **launch day**: the day the owner approves
this kit and the landing page is published. Nothing is posted until then.

Posting is manual. The X connector is not authenticated on the runner, and Reddit and Product
Hunt have no connector at all. Every post is in `posts.md`, every clip in `content/clips/`,
every image in `store/screenshots/en-US/`.

Before anything goes out: open the App Store link on a phone and check it resolves, and
check that the landing page is live at its published URL.

## Don't

- Don't post the same text to two subreddits on the same day.
- Don't post a competitor's screenshot. The before/after uses text for the "before".
- Don't quote any revenue figure. If one ever appears, it's modeled, and it says so.
- Don't promise that reports reach anyone. They stay on the phone until the reader sends the
  code.
