Letters to the desk

What people ask about trivia apps

Will ads show up after a few days?

No. There is no advertising SDK in the app, so there is nothing to switch on later. Every release is checked against the binary before it ships.

I paid for another trivia app and the ads got worse. Is Pro like that?

There are no ads to remove, so Pro doesn't remove any. It adds practice rounds and your accuracy by section. The daily edition is the same with or without it.

Do I run out of lives or need coins?

No. There are no lives, no energy, no coins and no gems. Nothing runs out, so there is nothing to wait for or buy back.

What if an answer is wrong?

Every question shows its explanation and its source, so you can check it yourself. If it still looks wrong, tap "something wrong here?" on that screen. It's kept under Corrections in Settings with a short code; send the code to support and the question is set right in the next pack.

Does everyone get the same ten?

Your first edition is the day's ten, the same as anyone else's first. After that Quizday sets your editions from the questions that caught you, and the mix gets harder as you file more, so yours becomes your own.

What kinds of questions are there?

Geography, history, science, nature, art and literature, music, film and TV, sport, food and drink, technology, space, language and general knowledge.

Is there a timer?

No. Take as long as you like over each question. A round usually takes a couple of minutes.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your streak starts again, and that's all. Nothing is lost, nothing is locked, and there's nothing to buy to get it back.

Does it work offline?

The daily edition and the late edition work completely offline. Only Pro's practice rounds use the network.

What do you collect about me?

Nothing. No account, no analytics, no trackers. Your scores stay on your phone. The privacy policy says the same thing at greater length.
