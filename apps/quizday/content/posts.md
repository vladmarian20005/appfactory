# Quizday, posts

Links: the App Store page,
https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quizday-daily-trivia-no-ads/id6810429464 (live since
29 September 2026), and the landing page published from `content/site/`. Images are the
composed store screenshots in `store/screenshots/en-US/` (web copies in `content/site/shots/`)
unless a post says otherwise. Clips are in `content/clips/`.

The voice is the app's night editor: dry, brisk, specific, quietly proud of the edition. He
talks in newsroom terms and doesn't gush. Outside the app he can say "no ads" plainly,
because the whole pitch is the thing the leaders get wrong. No "game-changer", no emoji
confetti, no "you're going to love".

Claims that are safe, all checked against the code and the listing: no ad SDK; no lives,
coins, gems or energy; 450 original questions, each with an explanation and a source; a
"something wrong here?" mark on every answer; the first edition is 4 easy, 4 medium, 2 hard and it hardens
with editions filed; the late edition opens at seven editions filed, eight questions at 25,
your choice of section at 60; no account, no analytics; network only in Pro's practice;
Pro is $2.99 per week or $19.99 per year with a 3-day free trial.

**Reports stay on the phone.** "Something wrong here?" saves the question to a Corrections
list in Settings with a code, and the reader sends that code to support herself. Nothing is
sent automatically, so never write "we read every report" or "it tells us".

**Don't say "the same ten for everyone"**. That stopped being true at the second polish pass: a reader's first edition
is the day's ten, and after that the desk sets editions from her misses.
