Ten trivia questions a day, dated and set like an edition of a paper. Play them in a couple of minutes, then you're done until tomorrow.

No ads. Not between questions, not after a wrong answer, not anywhere. No lives to wait for, no coins to buy, nothing that runs out and asks you for money to keep going.

EVERY ANSWER EXPLAINED
Miss one and you find out why, with a short explanation and the source it came from. You learn something even on the questions you get wrong. Think an answer is wrong? Report it from the same screen.

ONE EDITION A DAY
Today's edition arrives at midnight, dated, and is filed when you finish. Share your score as coloured squares — the numbers, not the answers.

THE DESK REMEMBERS
Quizday keeps track of what got past you and sets the next edition from it, so the same questions never come back in the same order. The sheet gets harder as you file editions too: four easy questions on your first, one by your hundredth. No two months of reading it are alike.

EARNED, NOT BOUGHT
File seven editions and the late edition opens: five more questions built from the ones that caught you. It costs nothing, it is not for sale, and nothing about it expires.

KEEP A STREAK
A calendar shows every day you played, shaded by how well you did. Current streak, best streak, days played. Set one gentle reminder if you want it, or none at all.

450 ORIGINAL QUESTIONS
Four hundred and fifty questions across nature, history, science, geography, film and TV, music, sport, food and technology, each with an explanation and a source. Written for Quizday, not scraped.

QUIZDAY PRO — OPTIONAL
The daily edition is free forever. Pro adds practice: unlimited rounds in any category, at easy, medium or hard, plus your accuracy broken down by category so you can see what you're weakest at. Pro adds practice, not access.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account. No sign-up. No analytics, no trackers, no advertising SDK. Your scores stay on your device. The only time Quizday uses the network is when you start a practice round.

Questions in practice mode come from the Open Trivia Database.
