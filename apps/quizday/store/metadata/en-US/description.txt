Ten trivia questions a day. The same ten for everyone, everywhere. Play them in a couple of minutes, then you're done until tomorrow.

No ads. Not between questions, not after a wrong answer, not anywhere. No lives to wait for, no coins to buy, nothing that runs out and asks you for money to keep going.

EVERY ANSWER EXPLAINED
Miss one and you find out why, with a short explanation and the source it came from. You learn something even on the questions you get wrong. Think an answer is wrong? Report it from the same screen.

ONE ROUND A DAY
Today's ten arrive at midnight and are the same for every player, so you can compare with a friend without spoiling anything. Share your score as coloured squares — the numbers, not the answers.

KEEP A STREAK
A calendar shows every day you played, shaded by how well you did. Current streak, best streak, days played. Set one gentle reminder if you want it, or none at all.

THIRTY ROUNDS OF ORIGINAL QUESTIONS
Three hundred questions across nature, history, science, geography, film and TV, music, sport, food and technology. Every round ramps from easy to hard.

QUIZDAY PRO — OPTIONAL
The daily ten is free forever. Pro adds practice: unlimited rounds in any category, at easy, medium or hard, plus your accuracy broken down by category so you can see what you're weakest at. Pro adds practice, not access.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account. No sign-up. No analytics, no trackers, no advertising SDK. Your scores stay on your device. The only time Quizday uses the network is when you start a practice round.

Questions in practice mode come from the Open Trivia Database.
