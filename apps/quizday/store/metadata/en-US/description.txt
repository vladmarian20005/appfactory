Quizday gives you ten trivia questions a day, dated and set like the day's edition of a paper. A round takes a couple of minutes, and then you're done until tomorrow.

There are no ads anywhere, including between questions and after a wrong answer. You never wait for lives or buy coins, and nothing runs out and then asks you to pay to keep going.

Every answer explained
When you miss a question, a short explanation tells you why and names the source it came from, so the ones you get wrong still teach you something. If you think an answer is wrong, you can report it from the same screen.

One edition a day
Each edition arrives at midnight with the date on it and is filed when you finish. You can share your score as a printed front page or as a row of red and white squares, and neither gives away the answers.

The desk remembers
Quizday keeps track of the questions that got past you and sets your next edition from them, so the same questions never come back in the same order. The sheet also gets harder as you file editions. Your first has four easy questions, and from edition 151 on there is one.

The late edition
After you file seven editions, the late edition opens: five more questions built from the ones that caught you. You earn it by playing. It can't be bought, and it doesn't expire.

Your streak
A calendar marks every day you played, shaded by how well you did, with your current streak, your best streak and the number of days you've played. You can set one daily reminder or leave it off.

450 original questions
The questions cover nature, history, science, geography, film and TV, music, sport, food and technology. Each one has an explanation and a source, and every one was written for Quizday, not scraped.

Quizday Pro (optional)
The daily edition is free and will stay free. Pro is for practice: unlimited rounds in any category at easy, medium or hard, plus your accuracy by category so you can see where you're weakest. Nothing in the daily edition is locked behind it.

Privacy
There's no account and no sign-up. Quizday has no analytics, no trackers and no advertising SDK, and your scores stay on your device. The app only uses the network when you start a practice round.

Practice questions come from the Open Trivia Database.
