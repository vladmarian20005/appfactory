Privacy Policy and Terms for Thousand

Effective 12 September 2026

1. Information we collect

None. Thousand has no sign-up, no login and no user profile. We do not collect your name, your email address, your location, your contacts, your photos or any device identifier. We operate no server that receives data from the app.

2. What the app stores on your device

Thousand keeps the following on your device, where it stays. It is included in your device backup if you use one, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

  • One row per word you have met: when it is next due, how many times it has come back clean, how many times it went back to the bench, and when it last went into the wall. This is what the schedule, the wall and the count of words known are drawn from.
  • Your settings: whether onboarding is done, whether the word is spoken when a tile turns, whether sounds are on, and whether the daily reminder is on and at what hour.
  • How many words new to you were introduced today, so the app can stop at its daily allowance instead of handing you the whole thousand at once.

3. Network use

Thousand makes no network requests. It works entirely offline.

4. Purchases

The Whole Wall is a one-time purchase, offered as a single one-time unlock. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see or receive your payment details. The drill, the schedule, the pronunciation, the wall and the progress count are free forever, with the first hundred words by frequency and the Everyday and People & family panels open from the start.

5. Notifications

One optional local reminder a day, off until you turn it on in Settings, scheduled on the device by UNUserNotificationCenter. It names the tiles waiting at the bench and never counts the days you were away. Nothing is sent from a server; there is no push token and no server to send one to.

6. Advertising, analytics and third-party code

Thousand contains no advertising SDK and no ads of any kind, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no tracking, and no data shared with data brokers. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt does not appear because there is nothing to ask about.

7. Children

Thousand is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects nothing, it holds no personal information about anyone, of any age.

8. Your control over your data

Everything Thousand stores is on your device. Erase it from within the app, or delete the app, and it is gone. There is no account to close and no server-side copy for us to delete, because there is no server. If you have a question about any of this, write to us at the address below.
