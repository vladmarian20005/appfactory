What it is

1,000 words, in order of use

The ones you actually meet, first.

The deck is ordered by how often a word turns up, not alphabetically and not by lesson number, and it is cut into twelve panels — everyday, people and family, food and drink, the house, city and travel, work and school, body and health, time and number, nature and weather, going and coming, feeling and mind, describing.

  • An example sentence for every single word, with its English translation.
  • Castilian pronunciation on every word, spoken by the phone itself.
  • Search that finds any tile without losing your place in the wall.

It remembers what you forget

Each word comes back as it starts to slip.

Turn the tile, then say how it went: Not yet, Got it, Easy. A spaced-repetition schedule on the phone decides when each word returns — sooner for the ones that slipped, much later for the ones you found easy. Nothing turns red. A word you missed is a tile going back for another firing.

  • When nothing is due, the app says so and stops. It never invents work.
  • No network requests at all — the underground, a plane, a bag on a train.
  • Nothing you drill leaves the phone.

The loop

Turn it over. Say how it went.

A session is forty seconds to ten minutes, depending on what is due. Read the Spanish, tap the tile, see the English with a sentence it lives in — then Not yet, Got it or Easy. When the last one is graded the bench is clear and the day's tiles go into the wall. Then the app stops.

Questions

Is there really no subscription?

No. One payment of $9.99 opens all 1,000 words and all twelve panels for good. There is no trial, no renewal and no second tier to buy later.

How much can I do without paying?

The drill, the schedule, the pronunciation, the wall and the count are free forever, with the first hundred words and two whole panels open from the start.

Does it work offline?

Completely. The whole deck ships inside the app and the pronunciation is spoken by the device, so it works on the underground, on a plane and where the signal drops.

Which Spanish is it?

Common-core vocabulary, useful in Spain and in Latin America. The spoken pronunciation is Castilian, from your iPhone's own Spanish voice.

Where does the pronunciation come from?

Your phone speaks it. That is why there is no bill and no network — and it is a synthesised voice, not a recorded speaker, which is the honest trade for both.

Is there a streak, or hearts, or gems?

None of them. The only number that changes is words known, of a thousand, and it cannot go down.

What data do you collect?

Nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no tracking and no server to send anything to. Your card states never leave the phone.

Which other languages?

Only Spanish for English speakers, on purpose — one language finished beats forty started. A second pair is planned for a later version, at no extra cost.

What if I get a new phone?

Restore the purchase from Settings with the same Apple Account. The deck reinstalls with the app; your card states live in the phone's own backup.
