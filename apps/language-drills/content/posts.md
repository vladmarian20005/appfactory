The voice outside the app is the setter's, one notch plainer: a tile-setter who has laid tile
for thirty years, talks about clay, glaze and the wall, and is pleased without performing it.
Outside the product he can say "no subscription" and "no streak" plainly, because that's the
pitch. No "game-changer", no "fluent in 30 days", no emoji confetti, no flags.

**Claims that are safe**, all checked against the code, STATUS.md and the listing:
- 1,000 common Spanish words for English speakers, in frequency order, in twelve themes.
- Every word has an example sentence and its English translation, written for this app.
- Spaced repetition on the phone: *Not yet / Got it / Easy*; three clean firings and a word is
  in the wall for good.
- Pronunciation is the phone's own Castilian voice (`es-ES`). It's synthesised, not a recorded
  speaker. Say so if anyone asks, never "native audio".
- No network requests at all, no account, no analytics, no ads.
- Free: the drill, the schedule, the pronunciation, the wall, the count, the first 100 words and
  two whole panels (Everyday, People & family). **$9.99 once** opens all 1,000 and all twelve
  panels. No trial, no subscription, nothing renews.
- When nothing is due it says "The bench is swept." and stops. It never invents work.

**Don't say:**
- "Native speaker audio", "AI", "fluent", or any number of days to fluency.
- "Works on iPad" (it's iPhone only), "syncs" (it doesn't), or "more languages free later".
  A second pair is planned; its price isn't decided.
- Any revenue figure. If one ever has to appear, it's **modeled**, and the post says so.
- That the deck is "verified by a linguist". It was checked mechanically (each example contains
  its word) and the owner spot-checks it, and that's all.
