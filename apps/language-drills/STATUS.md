## Where it stands

Planned from [issue #5](https://github.com/vladmarian20005/appfactory/issues/5) by the
build-request procedure, after the owner picked idea 3 from the 2026-09-12 scout brief. Verdict was
**build with a change**: one language pair bought once, rather than the bundled deck-per-language
that was pitched.

**Thousand** — 1,000 common Spanish words for English speakers, spaced repetition, pronunciation
from `AVSpeechSynthesizer`, no network at all, no streak, one-time $9.99 unlock with the first 100
words free.

## What the evidence could and could not say

Apple's review feed returned nothing for all five top apps in this niche (`feedEmpty: true`,
`reviewsSampled: 0` for Duolingo, Babbel, Rosetta Stone Classic, Memrise, EWA), and there is no
browser in the build sandbox. **There are no complaint quotes behind this spec.** The wedge rests
on the competitors' own store descriptions, on ratings, and on modeled revenue:

- $30M/mo modeled across 47 apps, Duolingo at 68%, but the other 46 still split $9.6M/mo modeled
  and the 15 tail apps named in the evidence all sit between $120K and $680K/mo modeled.
- Median rating across the 20 rated apps is 4.75★, with three below 4.6★ — a normal market.
- Lingvano (one language only) makes $480K/mo modeled at 4.88★, which is why the scope narrowed.

The open risk, recorded so the next stage does not lose it: the two youngest apps in the niche make
**$14K and $2.5K/mo modeled**. Newcomers are not breaking in. That is the kill criterion in
PLAN.md §11.
