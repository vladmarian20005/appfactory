# Plainfood, five short videos

Format: vertical 1080×1920, 20–30 seconds, captions burned in, no voice needed. Footage is a
simulator screen recording (`xcrun simctl io <udid> recordVideo`) with the sample data loaded
(`-onboarded -sampleData`). Hooks come from the leader's own reviews.

## 1. "They put macros behind a paywall. We didn't."
- 0–3s  Hook text over the Today screen: *MyFitnessPal put macros behind a paywall.*
- 3–8s  Cut to Plainfood Today: ring fills, macro bars visible. Text: *Protein, carbs, fat. Free.*
- 8–16s Tap + on Lunch, search "greek yogurt", pick, Add. Ring updates. Text: *Search. Scan. Type. Free.*
- 16–24s Open Settings → paywall. Zoom on the promise line. Text: *It says so on the paywall.*
- 24–28s End card: app icon, "Plainfood. Calories and macros. Free, always."

## 2. "Every update took something away"
- 0–4s  Hook: a review quote card: *"Every time I update it they take away a feature that used to be free."*
- 4–10s Plainfood Today screen. Text: *What is free today stays free.*
- 10–20s Show barcode Scan tab, then Manual entry. Text: *Barcode. Manual. Recent foods.*
- 20–28s End card with the promise line.

## 3. "Log lunch in 6 seconds"
- 0–2s  Hook: *Log lunch in 6 seconds.* Timer overlay starts.
- 2–8s  Tap +, type "chicken", tap result, tap Add. Timer stops at ~6s.
- 8–14s Ring and macros update. Text: *No account. No ads. No feed.*
- 14–20s End card.

## 4. "Pro is analysis, not access"
- 0–4s  Hook: *What does Pro actually cost you?*
- 4–12s Trends screen: 7 days, then 30 days. Text: *Trends, averages, streaks. That is Pro.*
- 12–20s Cut back to Today. Text: *Everything else stays free.*
- 20–26s End card with weekly and yearly prices and the trial.

## 5. "Your food log, on your phone"
- 0–4s  Hook: *Where does your food diary live?*
- 4–12s Airplane mode toggle on, open Plainfood, Today still works, add a manual food. Text: *On your device. Works offline for manual entry.*
- 12–20s Settings → Privacy page. Text: *No analytics. No tracking. Only food lookups leave the phone.*
- 20–26s End card.
