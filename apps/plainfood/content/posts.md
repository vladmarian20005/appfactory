## Reddit
**r/loseit** (text post, no link in body per sub rules; link in comments if asked)
Title: I got tired of calorie apps moving free features behind a paywall, so I built one that promises not to
Body: Long-time MFP user. When macros went Premium I looked at the alternatives and most had the same trajectory. I made Plainfood: search/barcode/manual entry, daily calories and macros, goals with presets, all free, with that promise written on the paywall. Pro is trends and averages only. Data is from Open Food Facts, so coverage of restaurant chains is thinner than MFP, which is the honest trade-off. Happy to answer questions about how the numbers are calculated.
