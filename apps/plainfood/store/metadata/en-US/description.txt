Plainfood is a calorie and macro tracker that keeps the basics free. Search a food, scan a barcode, or type it in. Your calories, protein, carbs and fat update on the home screen as you go. No account, no ads, no feed.

WHAT STAYS FREE, ALWAYS
• Food search powered by Open Food Facts, millions of products
• Barcode scanning
• Manual entry for home cooking
• Daily calories and macros against your goals
• Meals: breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks
• Goals with presets for maintain, cut and bulk
• Recent foods, so repeat logging takes two taps

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your food log lives on your device. Plainfood has no accounts and no analytics. The only network requests are food lookups.
