# Tidepour, five short videos

Vertical, 1080×1920, 30 fps, in `content/clips/`. Every clip is the app on an iPhone 17 Pro Max
simulator, played by the app itself through its `-demo` launch flags (nothing on a runner can
touch a screen), scaled to the full height and padded in the shallows' deep water `#0F3340`, so
the margins either side are the brand and the captions can sit in them.

Re-record with `node apps/color-sort/content/record.mjs` after `tools/sim.sh build` and one
`tools/sim.sh run` (to install it); the takes, their launch flags and the measured cuts are in
that file. Captions are not burned in: the timings below are clip time, in seconds.

Hooks come from the category's own reviews (SPEC.md, *Wedge*). Quote a review, never name the
app it came from. Caption type: New York bold for the hook and the end card, SF semibold
tracked uppercase for the small labels, Sea light `#E9F6F2` on the deep water, Mint `#4FE3D2`
for the one word that matters. No emoji.

Every video ends on the same end card for 2 s, laid over the last frame: the icon, **Tidepour**,
and "No ads. Every level solvable." (the subtitle) — then "Free on the App Store" once it is.
