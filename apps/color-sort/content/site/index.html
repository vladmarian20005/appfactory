The wedge

Every level has a solution

We check before you ever see it.

Boards are generated, not hand-made — and every one of them is solved before it is handed to you. Tidepour deals a board, searches it for a way to finish, and discards the board if there isn't one. Nothing you are given can be a dead end.

The length of that solution is par: the charted line, drawn as a rail you fill as you pour. Ask for a hint and the app points at the next move of a line it has already proved. Take your own route and it re-solves from the board in front of you, so the hint is right even once you have left the original line behind.

The deal

No ads. No coins. No lives.

Nothing here runs out.

There is no advertising SDK in this app at all — so there is nothing to skip, nothing to watch for a reward, and nothing to pay to switch off. No currency, no gems, no energy, no timer. You cannot get stuck behind a wall that a payment removes, because there is no wall.

Every move is written to disk the moment you make it. Closing the app, switching away or running out of battery never costs you a level — the rack is exactly where you left it.

Every evening

The same pool for everyone

A new one every day, offline.

One board a day, seeded from the date, so the puzzle you are working on is the puzzle everyone else opening Tidepour today is working on. Clear it and your streak grows; the month calendar lights the evenings you played.

Tidepour makes no network request at all — not for levels, not for scores, not for anything. It works the same on a plane, in the car and underground as it does at home.

Questions

The things people ask about this kind of game

Will I ever be handed a level that can't be finished?

No. Every board is solved by the app before it is dealt to you, and a board it cannot finish is discarded and re-dealt. That guarantee is the reason this app exists.

Are there ads between levels?

There are no ads anywhere. Not between levels, not on the board, not on launch. There is no advertising SDK in the binary at all.

Do I have to buy coins to get past a hard level?

There is no currency in the app — no coins, no gems, no extra slots to buy, no lives to refill. Progress cannot be purchased, so it cannot be withheld.

Is the $4.99 unlock a subscription?

No. It is a single non-consumable purchase with no renewal and no trial. The 60 free levels and the free daily puzzle are the trial.

Is there a timer?

No, and that is deliberate. There is no clock on any board and no deadline beyond the date itself. Take a minute or take an hour.

Does it work offline?

Completely. Tidepour makes no network requests of any kind, so it behaves identically with no signal.

I can't tell some colours apart. Can I still play?

Yes. The unlock adds an Okabe–Ito palette chosen to stay separable for every common kind of colour blindness, plus a distinct shape stamped on every unit so the rack reads without relying on colour at all. Every vial also reads out under VoiceOver as the colours it holds, from the bottom up.

What happens if the app closes in the middle of a level?

Nothing is lost. Every pour is saved to disk as you make it, so a crash, a kill or a flat battery returns you to exactly the rack you left.

What do you collect about me?

Nothing. No account, no sign-up, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no tracking. Your levels, your streak and your settings live on your device and nowhere else. The privacy policy says the same thing at greater length.
