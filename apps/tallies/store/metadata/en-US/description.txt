Count anything, on your phone, without an account.

Tap to add. Tap to take away. That is the whole app.

Most counter apps are one number on a plain screen with a banner ad across the bottom. The rest are habit trackers that want an email address before they will count to three. Tallies is neither. Keep several counters side by side, give each one a colour and a daily goal if you want one, and read back what the week actually looked like.

WHAT IT DOES

• Several counters at once, each with its name and running total on one screen
• A large plus on every row, so adding one never means opening anything first
• Tap a counter for the number in full size, a plus and a minus, and the last fortnight as a bar chart (the last week on the free plan)
• Set a daily goal and the counter draws a ring that fills as you go
• Swipe a counter away when you are done with it
• Reset today, or reset a counter completely — both ask before they do it
• History: every tap you have made, newest first, grouped by day, with a total for the week and the month beside each counter

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

• No account. There is no sign-up screen, because there is no server behind this.
• No ads. Not a banner, not a full-screen one, not a video to watch before you continue.
• No network. Tallies never connects to anything, and works exactly the same in airplane mode.
• No tracking, no analytics, no third-party kits of any sort.

Everything you count stays on this phone.

FREE AND PRO

Free: up to three counters, and the last seven days on each counter's chart.

Tallies Pro: as many counters as you like, the full fourteen-day chart, and the History screen across every counter.

Pro adds room, not access. Nothing you have already counted is ever locked away from you.

Tallies Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, $2.99 per week or $19.99 per year, each with a 3-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and you can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
