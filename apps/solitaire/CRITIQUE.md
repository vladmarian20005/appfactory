# Baize · critique

**Verdict: fail.** The direction is the best this factory has written — a blue baize under one lamp, cream serif cards, a clerk who keeps a diary — and the code for the drag, the sweep, the fans, the ladder and the chooser is all there and matches it. But the pack the whole app deals from, `ios/App/deals.json`, was never generated or committed, so `Pack.load()` returns `.empty`, every `lay()` fails on its guard, and not one capture of this run shows a card on the table: the table, the win, the first deal, both filmstrips and all four ladder panels are the same screen, and that screen says "Today's is in the diary" to a player whose diary is empty.

## Slop tells present

`node tools/design/tells.mjs solitaire`: 0 hard tells, 0 smells. The screenshots:

- **Nothing moves.** `qa/design/moment-move.png` and `qa/design/moment-win.png` are identical frame to frame (the move strip's first two frames are blank white — the app had not drawn yet).
- **The ladder is interchangeable.** `qa/design/ladder.png`: rung 5, 51, 150 and 500 differ only in the printed number; no grade, draw, mark, gilt edge, seal or claret cloth is visible, because no table is.
- **A false done state.** `TableView.swift:76-78` shows `DoneToday` whenever `table == nil && won == nil`, which is also what a failed `lay()` leaves behind (`Desk.swift:100-101`, `:108`). On a fresh install (`qa/05-first-deal.png`) the clerk says today's is in the diary; `qa/01-diary.png` says nothing is written. Both captures are from the same run.
- **System large titles.** `DiaryView.swift:37`, `DrawerView.swift:19` and the kit's Settings render "Diary", "Drawer", "Settings" in black SF Pro on the blue baize in light mode (`qa/01-diary.png`, `qa/06-drawer.png`, `qa/09-settings.png`). DESIGN.md §Type promised New York for every display element.
- **The first App Store screenshot is an empty state.** `qa.json` names `01-diary` as the 4.3 screenshot and passes `-sampleData`; the capture shows "Nothing written yet", because `Desk.simulate(to:)` (`Desk.swift:536-537`) breaks out of its loop on the first nil deal and seeds no entries.

## The second session

- **Where the curve stops:** `Play.swift:10-14` — `grade` reaches 9 at rung 113, `draw` turns to three at 40, `tight` reaches 35 at 169, so `Ladder.flattensAt` is 169 and `climbs(through: 150)` is true. Honest, stated in DESIGN.md, and past it the chooser still varies the shape against the record. In this build the ladder is unreachable: `Play.ledgerDeal` (`Play.swift:49-52`) returns nil at every rung because `pack.pool` is empty.
- **What chooses the next unit:** `Play.choose` (`Play.swift:71-86`) — the shape `Mastery.next` picks from the record, at the ladder's draw and grade, skipping `record.played`, then nearest grade, then a seeded second pass. Today's is keyed by day number (`Play.swift:98-105`) and never `%`. Written on every deal out or abandoned (`Desk.swift:142-145`, `:333-337`). Correct design; no content to choose from.
- **What is at risk and what losing it costs:** `Run` per deal (`Desk.swift:165`, `:221`): a take-back costs the chain and this deal's clean sheet, and the mark (`Play.swift:41-43`) is a second honour; `takeBack` never touches entries, rung or tomorrow. Correct, and unreachable.
- **What is earned without paying:** `Play.earned` (`Play.swift:110-121`) — claret at 10 and the brass lattice at 25 gate `Record.clothInUse` and `backInUse` (`Record.swift:75-89`) on `hasEarned`, not on `isPro`; gilt at 50, the seal at 100, volume two at 365. Real, not a purchase. The drawer labels them `EARNED AT 10` and `EARNED AT 25` (`qa/06-drawer.png`).

Why someone opens this on Thursday: as shipped, they cannot play on Monday. With the pack in place the answer is the one DESIGN.md gives — a shared deal everyone else is also sitting down to, a clean sheet and a mark to protect, and the ledger's next number waiting in the shape the record says you are worst at — and that answer is already written in `Voice.ending` and `Voice.nextLine`.

## Keep

- The direction, all of it. The blue cloth with its nap and lamp, walnut, brass corners, cream card stock with New York indexes, one big pip, the court emblems, radius 8. The icon is a picture of the table, not a hand of aces.
- The four art pieces (`Desk`, `Fans`, `Diary`, `Drawer`) and the way onboarding matches its mock.
- `CardsView.swift`: the lift at 1.04 with the grown shadow, the trailing spring per card, the nudge and the drop ghost, the shrug, the ghost hand, the row-by-row deal. `Desk.feedback`: `.step(rank − 1)` on a card going up, the 80 ms turn tick, no buzzer anywhere.
- The sweep and `WinView`: fans by tier, pips in card colours, the diary line with the week strip.
- `Play.swift` whole: the ladder, the chooser, the dailies, `Earned`. `Voice.swift` whole.
- The clerk's copy everywhere it is true: "Nothing written yet", "Lay today's deal", "Open the drawer", "Leave it shut", "Gather the cards".
- Dark mode. The drawer's count on card stock with the mastery capsule per shape.

## Fix, in this order

1. **Generate and commit the pack.** `node apps/solitaire/tools/pack.mjs` writes `apps/solitaire/ios/App/deals.json` (ARCHITECTURE.md says it is committed; it never was). Its self-test proves deals in tens to hundreds of milliseconds each, so the full pack is a few minutes on a runner; `--limit` first if you want to see the pipeline move. Then open the built `Baize.app` and confirm `deals.json` is inside it — XcodeGen folds non-Swift files under `App` in as resources, but check rather than trust. The next capture shows it is fixed when `qa/03-table.png` has cards on it, `qa/05-first-deal.png` shows No. 1 with the ghost hand mid-curve, and `TableFoot` prints a seed line.
2. **Make the done state truthful.** `TableView.swift:76-78`: render `DoneToday` only when `desk.todayState` is `.out`; in every other `table == nil` case show the table's head with "Lay today's deal" as the prominent action and nothing claiming a finished day. In `Desk.lay`, a nil from `todayDeal`/`ledgerDeal` should `assertionFailure("pack empty")` in debug so this can never pass verify silently again. Fixed when a `-reset` launch never says "Today's is in the diary".
3. **Recapture everything that depends on a deal.** With the pack in the bundle, `-sampleData` seeds the 23-entry October page (`Desk.simulate`), `-won` reaches `write()`, `-demo move` plays twelve moves and a take-back, `-demo win` plays the sweep, and `-rung 5|51|150|500` lays different tables. Fixed when: `01-diary` is the mock-4 page with buried cards and `23 ENTRIES · 9 DAYS RUNNING`; `02-win` shows four fans, `118` at 96 pt and "It came out clean"; `moment-move.png` shows a run lifted, a card turned, a card up, a take-back; `moment-win.png` shows cards flying home then fans; `ladder.png` shows `DRAW ONE · GRADE 1`, `DRAW THREE · GRADE 4`, grade 9 on claret with gilt edges, and grade 9 with the seal.
4. **Brand the large titles.** "Diary", "Drawer" and "Settings" are black SF Pro on blue (`qa/01-diary.png`, `qa/06-drawer.png`, `qa/09-settings.png`). Set them in New York Semibold in `ink`: in `Baize.swift`, `UINavigationBar.appearance().largeTitleTextAttributes` and `titleTextAttributes` with the serif descriptor and `UIColor(brand.palette.ink)`, or replace `.navigationTitle` with a principal toolbar `Text("Diary").brandFont(.largeTitle)` the way `TableView` already does for its inline title. Show the name once the owner can use it (pro or earned) and keep `EARNED AT n` only while it is locked. Minor, and mostly invisible once fix 3 seeds the record.
