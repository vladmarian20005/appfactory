## The second session

- **Where the curve stops:** `Play.swift:10-14` — `grade` last moves at rung 113, `draw` at 40, `tight` at 169; `Ladder.flattensAt` is 169 and `climbs(through: 150)` is true. But the curve is bounded by supply, not only by the dials: `deals.json` holds 200 deals per grade at draw three and ~67 at draw one, and `Play.choose` at `Play.swift:77-82` takes the nearest grade once the ladder's grade is exhausted. Grade 9 is spent at rung ~313, grade 8 at ~499, and the ledger then descends one grade per ~200 rungs until the whole pool is played and `secondPass` (`:88-92`) begins near rung 1,900. `ladder.png` records it: rung 500 is `DRAW THREE · GRADE 7`.
- **What chooses the next unit:** `Play.shapeOrder` (`Play.swift:62-69`) asks `Mastery.next` for the weakest shape avoiding the last two, then `choose` (`:71-86`) takes the first unplayed deal at the ladder's draw and grade in that shape, skipping `record.played` (`Desk.swift:111`). Mastery is written on every deal out (`Desk.swift:333-337`), gathered (`:237`) or laid over (`:142-145`). Today's is `pack.dailies[day]` (`Play.swift:99`), never `%`. The weakness: at grade 9 draw three the pool is 166 Buried, 17 In the stock, 9 Long passes, 8 Kings up and 0 Even, so the shape dimension — the one DESIGN.md says keeps varying past 169 — is nearly single-valued where it matters most.
- **What is at risk and what losing it costs:** `Run` per deal — `hit()` on every card up (`Desk.swift:165`, `:293`), `miss()` on a take-back (`:221`) and a gather (`:241`); the mark is `line × (140 − tight)/100` (`Play.swift:41-43`) and shown in the head. A take-back changes the caps and sets a near-miss line (`:228`) and touches nothing else: `write()` is the only place entries or the rung change (`:339-342`), and it runs only when the deal is out.
- **What is earned without paying:** `Play.earned` (`Play.swift:110-121`) — the diary at 1, claret at 10, the brass lattice at 25, gilt edges at 50, the seal at 100, volume two at 365. `Record.clothInUse` and `backInUse` (`Record.swift:75-89`) open claret and the lattice on `hasEarned` or pro; `gilt` and `sealed` (`:91-92`) are earned only and cannot be bought. The table's menu offers the cloth once claret is earned (`TableView.swift:101-103`); `qa/03-table.png` shows the gilt edges at 60 entries.

Why someone opens this on Thursday: Thursday's deal is the week's first at draw three, grade 4, the same cards for everyone who sits down to it; the clean sheet and the mark are only ever this evening's to lose; No. 51 is waiting in the ledger in the shape the record says is weakest; and the seal is forty entries away. All of that is on the page and in the ending already — except the shape, which the player is never shown.

## Fix, in this order

1. **Stop the ladder descending past rung ~313.** `Play.choose` (`Play.swift:77-82`) falls to the nearest grade when the ladder's grade at the ladder's draw is spent; with 200 grade-9 draw-three deals in `deals.json` that is rung ~313, and `ladder.png` already shows rung 500 at `GRADE 7`. Two parts. In `choose`, when `unplayed` has nothing at the ladder's draw and grade, go straight to a per-grade second pass — `secondPass(draw:grade:)` restricted to that grade, ordered by `seed ^ rung` as it is now, skipping the 100 most recent `played` ids — rather than to an easier grade; a grade-9 deal she played ten months ago is a harder table than a fresh grade 7. And in `apps/solitaire/tools/pack.mjs`, weight the pack toward where the ladder lives: 600 at grade 9 draw three, 300 at grade 8, and let grades 1–3 at draw three go to 100 (the ladder is at draw one until rung 40 and only the Thursday–Sunday dailies use low draw-three grades). Then correct DESIGN.md's "Rung 300 and rung 900 are both the hardest table the pack has" to what is true. Fixed when the rung-500 panel of `ladder.png` reads `DRAW THREE · GRADE 9`.

2. **Let the player see what the app chose, and give it a real choice at the top.** Nothing on the table says the deal was laid against her record. In `TableHead.right` (`TableView.swift:165`) make the caps `DRAW THREE · GRADE 9 · BURIED` for ledger and loose deals (shape names: `BURIED`, `IN THE STOCK`, `KINGS UP`, `LONG PASSES`, `EVEN`), and in `Desk.lay(.ledger)` set one clerk line through `say` when `Mastery` picked a shape she has missed: "Laid against the record: a buried ace. Then fix the supply: `pack.mjs` cuts grades by rank over the whole pool, so grade 9 is 83 % Buried and has no Even deal; rank within each shape instead (grade 9 = the hardest ninth *of each shape*), so every grade has all five and `Mastery.next` has something to choose between at rung 400. Fixed when `qa/03-table.png`'s head shows a shape and the drawer's count table has five rows with entries at grade 9.
