A writing desk with a blue baize top, late, under one lamp. Today's deal is laid out on the cloth, and a diary lies open beside it where every deal that came out is written down.

Baize is Klondike solitaire, one deal a day, the same cards for everyone who sits down to it.

NO ADS. EVER.
There is no advertising SDK in this app. No pop-ups, no videos, no website opening in the middle of a hand. No coins, no lives, no energy, and nothing that runs out.

EVERY DEAL COMES OUT
Every deal in Baize was played through by a solver before the app was built, and only deals it proved winnable are bundled. The deal's number is printed on the table so you can see exactly which one it was. Nothing is rigged, and nothing changes with how much you have played.

WORKS WITH NO INTERNET
Baize makes no network connection at all. There is nothing to load, nothing to time out, no daily challenge that breaks behind a VPN, and no forced update in the middle of a hand.

TODAY'S DEAL
Today's deal runs a published week, like a crossword: gentle on Monday at draw one, draw three from Thursday, and the hardest of the pack on Sunday.

THE LEDGER
Your own numbered run of deals, which climbs as you play: harder deals, draw three from No. 40, and a mark that tightens toward the solver's own line. The next deal in the ledger is chosen from the kind of deal you find hardest — buried aces, aces in the stock, kings in the way.

PLAY IT CLEAN
Undo is free and unlimited. The only thing at stake is whether a deal came out clean, without taking a card back, and under the mark. That costs tonight's deal and nothing else.

THE DIARY
Every deal that comes out is written into the month as the card you had to dig for. A claret cloth, a brass lattice back, gilt edges and the clerk's seal arrive for playing, never for paying. Send the card or the whole page to a friend.

Drag or tap to play, four-colour suits, large pips, an optional clock, and a calm dark mode under the lamp. Accessibility is never behind the purchase.

THE DRAWER — ONE PAYMENT
Today's deal, the whole ledger and loose deals are free, forever. One payment of $4.99 opens the drawer: more card backs and cloths, the left-handed table, every past day's deal to lay again, and the whole count by draw, grade and kind of deal. No subscription.

Offline and private: Baize collects no data.
