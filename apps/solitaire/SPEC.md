# Baize

Planned 2026-10-12 from issue #10 ("build it" on the scout brief of 2026-10-12). Full plan with
evidence in appmonkey `plans/solitaire/PLAN.md`; this is the compression `app-build` reads.

## Promise

One Klondike deal a day, the same cards for everyone, every deal proved winnable before you see
it. Opens instantly, works with no internet at all, and never shows an ad. For people who play
solitaire to calm down and have deleted the big ones because they freeze, force-update and
hijack the screen.

## Wedge

From the top five's last 100 reviews each (evidence 2026-10-12; the leader is 61% unhappy,
⋆Solitaire 74%, Solitaire.com 62%):

1. **Ads that break or hijack the game.** "It takes you to a website I DO NOT wish to open. This
   should be illegal!" and "Worst ads possible. Threats to close bank accounts and accusations of
   downloading porn. I had to delete the app." (⋆Solitaire); "I spend more time watching ads than
   playing the game." (Solitaire.com) → No ad SDK in the binary.
2. **An app that does not work.** Themes *freeze* (7), *unplayable* (4) on the leader: "This app
   freeze CONSTANTLY when you're in the middle of a game… UNPLAYABLE!" and "Won't load daily
   challenge after update with VPN enabled."; "The game closes in the middle of play" (Disney)
   → No network code at all. Nothing to time out, gate on a VPN, or force an update for.
3. **Deals that feel rigged, and currency.** "It's easy to observe that the cards you get are
   rigged based on how much you've played vs how much you've spent" (Disney); "I have it set on
   winning every game, but it is almost impossible to do" (Spider) → Every deal is proved
   winnable by the build's solver, and its seed is printed on the table. No coins, no lives.

Match what they love: the winnable daily — "I enjoy the daily game which is guaranteed to be
winnable" (⋆Solitaire) — and the calm, dark-friendly table (*relaxing*, 6 on Disney; "I
recommend the night mode, it's so much cuter cards", on the leader).

Deliberately skipped: the meta-game. No postcards, levels, XP, collections or farms.

## MVP

Three screens, one loop: open, play the day's deal, watch the month fill in.

1. **Table** — the deal. Klondike, draw-1 or draw-3, drag or tap-to-place, unlimited undo,
   restart, no timer unless asked. The seed printed small at the edge. Win = the kit's
   celebration (`confetti`, `CountUp`, `Haptics.celebrate`, `Tones`). Nothing interrupts a hand.
2. **Month** — the calendar of daily deals, which ones you cleared, the current streak, moves and
   time per day, share as a plain text line. Daily reminder toggle (local notification).
3. **The Deck (Pro)** — card backs and felts, left-handed layout, the archive of past daily
   deals, lifetime stats.

Plus the kit's Settings (restore, rate, share, support, privacy) and the paywall whose promise is:
no ads ever, nothing to run out of.

**Content is generated by the build, not written.** A `tools/` script seeds a shuffle, proves it
winnable by search, and writes a bundled `deals.json` of about 400 verified daily seeds plus a
free-play pool. The app only reshuffles from a seed, so a hand deals instantly and the phone
never runs the search. If the search cannot prove enough seeds in build time, fall back to
dealing backwards from a solved foundation, which is winnable by construction.

Local-first: `@AppStorage` for streak and settings, SwiftData for per-deal results. No network
code anywhere in the app.

Free forever: today's deal, unlimited winnable free play, undo, restart, the month calendar,
streak, share, night mode, large pips and colour-blind suit markers. Accessibility is never
behind the unlock.

## Monetization

**One non-consumable unlock, $4.99.** StoreKit 2. Product id `com.starhiveconcept.solitaire.unlock`.
Not a subscription, so guideline 3.1.2's Terms-of-Use block does not apply.
No trial: the free daily deal and unlimited free play are the trial, forever.
Unlock buys card backs and felts, the left-handed layout, the archive of past dailies and
lifetime stats. Restore in Settings.

All five competitors are free with ads and three also sell a subscription, so there is no
competitor price to match.

## Distinct from the leader

Not a reskin. MobilityWare's Solitaire is an ad-supported, network-dependent app with a no-ads
subscription, Daily Challenges behind a connection, and 61% of recent reviewers reporting
freezes and forced updates. Baize has no ad SDK, no network code, no account and no currency.
Its mechanic — one shared daily deal, proved winnable before it ships, with the seed visible and
a month calendar as the main progress surface — is in none of the top five. The table is drawn in
the app's own brand, not green felt.

## Risks

- **App Review 4.3.** Solitaire is the most reskinned genre on the store. Answered by the daily
  proved-winnable deal, the month calendar and a table with a look of its own — DESIGN.md owes
  this app that look before any Swift. A generic green felt gets rejected; the first screenshot
  shows the calendar and the seed, not a card table like the other forty-six.
- **The solver's reach.** A smaller pack if the search is slow; the deal-backwards fallback
  guarantees the pack either way.
- **A leader people do like.** 4.73★ across 2,066,452 ratings. We are taking the unhappy 61%.
- Licensed content, regulated claims, hardware, backend, network: none. Game flag: one mechanic,
  single player, a pack the build writes, board in SwiftUI, no ads, no lives, no currency.
