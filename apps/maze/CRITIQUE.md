# Lacework · critique

**Verdict: pass.** A lacemaker's pillow by a window, drawn all the way through: parchment on linen, steel pins, one indigo thread, New York for the numerals, and a lacemaker who talks about the piece and never about you — and, unusually for this factory, a book that reads its own ladder and a record that chooses the next ground. The thing that most decides it is that the play holds up as well as the look: the ladder is honest and still climbing at 150, the next pattern is chosen from what he worked badly, an unpick costs only tonight's clean sheet, and seven things arrive for winding rather than paying. What keeps the lines at 4 rather than 5 is craft on the win, one hazard in the first minute, and a filmstrip that cannot show the winding.

## Slop tells present

None. `tells.mjs --strict`: 0 hard tells, 0 smells. Nothing on the card is a checkmark, a heart, an arrow or a lightbulb; no screen is a `List` of `Label`s; the only red in the app is the kit's destructive "Empty the workbox" row (`07-workbox`), which is system chrome.

## The second session

- **Where the curve stops.** `Play.swift:10-15` — `Ladder.flattensAt` is 221, when `loose` reaches 2; `side` stops at rung 109, `open` at 85, `shape` at 165. Past 221 the shape is fixed at 14×14, fully open, a ground of windows, no end pinned, and what still changes is the ground (from the record) and where its windows fall (`Generator.swift:92-131`). The daily is a fixed weekly cycle, 6 → 131, seeded from the date (`Play.swift:31,60-90`); it never repeats a pattern and never climbs past Sunday's 131.
- **What chooses the next unit.** `Play.swift:96-99` — `mastery.next(from: groundsOpen(at: rung), count: 1, unseenShare: 0.3, avoiding: last three)`, read from a `Mastery<Ground>` that `Bench.swift:379-383` writes on every lift (≤1 unpick correct, ≥3 or a restart a miss, two records nothing). The pattern itself is seeded from `(rung, salt)` (`Play.swift:104`), so it never comes round; today's ground is a seeded draw avoiding the last three days (`Play.swift:66-70`). No `%` over a list and no uniform draw anywhere content is chosen; the only `randomElement` is the lacemaker's line (`Voice.swift:53`), from pools that never repeat back to back.
- **What is at risk.** One `Run` per `SavedPillow` (`Record.swift:158`): `hit()` per pin (`Bench.swift:229`), `miss()` once per unpick gesture (`Bench.swift:292`), the tier from `run.tier(score: longestChain, beating: bestThread)` (`Bench.swift:367`). An unpick costs this pattern's clean sheet and this thread; the rung still advances (`Bench.swift:390`), the piece still goes in the sampler, and nothing can be bought back. "Pull the pins" costs the snips and a mastery miss (`Bench.swift:320-331`, `LiftView.swift:30`).
- **What is earned without paying.** `Play.swift:118-133` — sampler at 1, rose silk at 6, the marking pin at 15, gold thread and picots at 40, indigo ticking at 100, initials at 200, the year cloth at 365, all on pieces worked, read at `Record.swift:52` and used in the workbox, the pillow's menu, the card, the lift and the sampler. The paywall opens the book past 60 and loose work (`Bench.swift:88,118-125`); it is a door, not the only one.

Why open it on Thursday: because Thursday's pattern is eight by eight in a ground nobody has seen yet, the same for everyone, and pattern 52 in the book is waiting in the ground the record says he is worst at, and gold thread is nineteen pieces away.

## Fix, in this order

1. **The picot edge escapes the card in the win** (`02-win`, `dark-02-win`, `ax-02-win`). `LiftView.swift:113-114` scales the lace 1.06 and raises it 28 pt, and `drawPicot` (`ThreadPath.swift:163-186`) spans the whole card from `inset − 0.1 pitch`, so the scallops cross the parchment's top edge and the pillow's. Draw the picot round the thread's own extent — the bounding box of the pins on `piece.path`, plus 0.45 pitch — so the piece lifts as a piece and its edge stays on the card; keep the 28 pt rise. The next capture shows gold scallops with parchment visible on all four sides, as in `mock-2-win`.
2. **A weekend install hands a newcomer the week's hardest pattern.** `Bench.swift:67-72` defaults `which = .today` with no first-launch case; `Play.swift:31` makes Saturday an 11×11 medallion and Sunday 14×14 with a window and a loose finish. When `record.pieces.isEmpty && record.rung == 1`, open on `.book` (pattern 1: 5×5 tulle, both ends pinned, two fifths open) and let the ending's second line say "Today's is pinned on the pillow: eight by eight, the spider ground." The next `05-first-pattern` (taken with `-reset`) shows a five-by-five.
3. **"Send a swatch" is under the tab bar on the win.** `LiftView.swift:186-189`'s `ViewThatFits` falls to the VStack because both children ask for infinite width; the mock has both buttons side by side and the build hides the second under the glass. In the HStack give `nextButton` `.frame(maxWidth: .infinity)` and the share link its natural width (or `.layoutPriority(1)` on the share link), and pad the ScrollView's bottom by the tab bar. The next `02-win` shows both buttons above the tab bar.
4. **The lift's head at accessibility sizes.** `ax-02-win`: "EIGHT BY EIGHT · SPIDER GROUND" in four lines beside "SEP-TEMBER 24". `LiftView.swift:39-43` is a bare HStack; `PillowView.swift:112-123` already has the `ViewThatFits` that stacks the two at large text. Reuse it. The next `ax-02` has the caps in one column, no hyphen, and the card higher on the screen.
5. **Name tomorrow.** `Voice.swift:97` ends today's piece on "Today's is in the sampler. Tomorrow's is pricked at midnight." and `PillowView.swift:216-224` says "The next pattern in the book is pinned and waiting." The week is published (`Play.swift:31`); say "Tomorrow's is nine by nine, pricked at midnight." and, on the done screen, "Tomorrow's is eleven by eleven, a medallion." It costs one `Play.dials(at: Play.dailyRung(day: tomorrow))`. The next `02-win` shows the size in the second line.
6. **The films miss the motion.** `moment-wind.png` is 13 identical frames of 16 and `moment-lift.png` 11 of 12: `qa.json` delays 1.6 s and 2.4 s, the demo sleeps 1.5 s before its first pin (`Bench.swift:555`) and then winds at 0.23 s a pin (`Bench.swift:588`), so the strip starts as the demo ends. Slow the wind demo to 0.45 s a pin and set the wind film's `delay` to 1.5 and `interval` to 0.12; set the lift film's `delay` to 1.9 so its twelve frames at 0.14 s cover the 1.3 s from the last pin to the margin card. The next strips show the reach, the wrap, the tighten, the pins out and the rise.
7. **Capture the sampler with today lifted.** `01-sampler` is the store's first screenshot by the spec's 4.3 note and it shows a "Back to the pillow" button where `mock-4-sampler` shows today's piece with "Worked clean, in one thread" in madder. Add `-won` to `01-sampler`'s args in `qa.json` (the lift sets `todayPiece`, and `SamplerView.swift:77-95` then draws the piece card and the tier line). The next `01-sampler` matches the mock's hero.
