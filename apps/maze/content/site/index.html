Today's pattern

A pattern a day, kept as lace

The same pattern for everyone, every morning.

Today's pattern is pricked from the date, so the card on your pillow is the card on everyone's. It runs a week like a crossword: five pins a side on Monday, fourteen with a window cut in it by Sunday. It is free every day, and it always will be.

Every piece you finish goes into your sampler, drawn in the thread you wound it with, newest first. It lives on your phone and it does not reset. A day you work gets a pin on the month card; a day you don't is a prick with no pin in it, and nothing is said about it.

The winding

One thread through every pin

Never crossing, never twice. Just look.

Draw the thread from the brass pin to a neighbour, and on, and on. It wraps each pin it turns on and sinks it into the pillow; a straight run of four tightens into a plait. Each pin has a small click under the finger, and a long bar plays a rising phrase.

Nothing hurries you. There is no clock. If the thread goes wrong, pick it out — free, as often as you like. The only thing at stake is whether tonight's piece was worked clean.

The pattern book

Proved to have one answer

The book climbs to fourteen pins a side.

Every pattern is made on your phone and checked by a solver before you see it. A board the solver cannot prove has exactly one way through is never pricked. You reach the answer by reading the card, not by guessing.

The book starts at five pins a side and grows to fourteen, then cuts medallions and windows, then lets go of its pinned ends so you have to find where the thread begins. The next pattern is built in the lace ground your own record says you find hardest.

Questions

What people ask about maze games

Will ads start showing up after level ten?

No. There is no advertising SDK in the app at all, so there is nothing to switch on later, and the check every release goes through fails the build if one ever appears.

Do I have to watch anything to keep playing?

No. There are no lives, no energy, no coins and no hints to buy. Nothing runs out, so nothing has to be refilled.

Will my progress reset?

Your sampler, your place in the book and a pattern half wound are saved on your phone as you go. Today's pattern is worked out from the date, so it cannot drift or reset, and a piece you have lifted stays lifted.

Is there really only one answer?

Yes. Every pattern is checked by a solver before you see it, and one that cannot be proved to have exactly one way through is thrown away. A pattern with no pinned ends counts a thread wound backwards as the same lace.

Does it get hard?

It starts gentle and it does not stay there. By the end of the week, and deep in the book, a pattern is fourteen pins a side with windows cut in it and no end pinned, so the first minute goes on finding where the thread can begin.

I've never played one of these. Where do I start?

The first time you open it, three small practice cards show you the idea in about half a minute. Then you start on the book's first pattern, five pins a side, whatever day it is.

Is the $4.99 a subscription?

No. It is a single purchase with no renewal and no trial. Today's pattern and the first sixty in the book are free without it.

Does it work offline?

Completely. Lacework makes no network connection of any kind. It works the same on a plane as it does in bed.

Can I play with VoiceOver?

Yes. Every pin reads out its row, column and whether it is bare, and a double tap on a neighbouring pin winds the thread onto it.

What do you collect about me?

Nothing. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Everything lives on your phone. The privacy policy says the same thing at greater length.
