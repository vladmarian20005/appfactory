# Lacework, launch schedule

Day 0 is the day App Review approves it. Before anything goes out that day, put the App Store
id in `ios/App/AppInfo.swift` (`appStoreID`) and swap the landing page's "Coming soon" badge
for the link. Every post below that says [link] wants the App Store URL, not the page.

Posting is manual: the X connector is not authenticated.

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Day 0, 09:00 | Landing page live with the App Store link. X post 10 (launch). Launch email. |
| Day 0, 12:00 | Reddit r/iOSGaming (developer post). Product Hunt scheduled for the next morning, 00:01 PT. |
| Day 0, evening | X post 2 with image 02 (the lift). Answer every Reddit comment. |
| Day 1 | Product Hunt live; post the maker's first comment at launch. Video 1 ("They promised no ads. Then level ten."). |
| Day 2 | X thread (post 5). Video 5 ("Maze games are all neon. This isn't."). Reddit r/iosapps. |
| Day 3 | X posts 3 and 7. Reddit r/puzzles, a bare puzzle, no link. |
| Day 4 | Video 3 ("Watch 10 ads to keep playing? No."). X post 8. |
| Day 5 | X posts 1 and 9. Video 4 ("Every maze here has exactly one answer."). |
| Day 6 | X posts 4 and 6. |
| Day 7 | `/retro maze` against the day-7 bar (100 downloads, 3 paying, rating ≥ 4.5). Then video 2 ("Got to level 70. Reset to 1.") or a repeat of whichever hook drew the most replies. |

The press blurb goes to anyone who asks, and with any pitch the owner chooses to send. Nothing
is sent to press automatically.

The videos need recording first (see `videos.md`, *Recording*). If they are not recorded by
day 1, ship the stills instead: the lift (02) for video 1's slot and the sampler (01) for
video 5's, with the hook as the post text.
