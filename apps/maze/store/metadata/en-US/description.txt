A lacemaker's pillow by a window. A pattern pricked into a card and pinned to the bolster, one bobbin of thread in your hand — and the thread wound from pin to pin until every pin is taken. Then the pins come out, and the piece lifts off the pillow as lace.

Lacework is a one-line maze puzzle. Draw a single unbroken thread through every pin on the card. It never crosses itself, it never goes through the gimp, and it takes every pin exactly once.

NO ADS. EVER.
There is no advertising SDK in this app, and there never will be. No currency, no lives, no energy, no hints to buy, nothing to watch, and nothing that runs out.

A PATTERN A DAY
Today's pattern is pricked from the date, so it is the same one for everyone who opens it. It runs a week like a crossword: gentle on Monday, fourteen pins a side with a window cut in it by Sunday. Today's pattern is free, every day, forever.

EVERY BOARD HAS EXACTLY ONE ANSWER
Every pattern is generated on your phone and proved by a solver before it is served. A board the solver cannot prove is never pricked. You can reach the answer by looking, never by guessing.

WORK IT CLEAN
Nothing hurries you. There is no clock. The only thing at stake is whether a piece was worked clean — wound in one go, never picked out. Unpicking is free and unlimited; it simply costs this piece its clean sheet, and nothing else.

A PATTERN BOOK THAT KEEPS CLIMBING
The book grows from five pins a side to fourteen, opens into medallions and windows, and lets go of its pinned ends. The next pattern is chosen from the lace grounds you have found hardest.

A SAMPLER THAT KEEPS
Every finished piece goes into your sampler, drawn in the thread you wound it with. Rose silk, a marking pin, gold thread with picot edges and more arrive for playing, never for paying. Your progress lives on your phone and never resets.

Send a swatch of the day's piece to a friend without giving the answer away.

THE PATTERN BOOK — ONE PAYMENT
Today's pattern and the first sixty in the book are free. One payment of $4.99 opens every pattern past the sixtieth, loose work (a fresh pattern whenever you want one, never the same twice), and the ledger of your pieces by size and by ground. No subscription.

Offline, untimed and private: Lacework makes no network connection and collects no data.
